Minutes of Meeting

Lessons Learned library. A lesson is its own record. It can be captured on its own, or linked to the issue or the meeting action item that produced it — and it stays in this library after that issue closes, which is the point of keeping it.
Minutes of Meeting. The record of what was said, plus the action items it produced. Raise an action item to put it in the Issues & Concerns register and link the two — after that the register is where it is chased, and these minutes keep saying what the meeting said.
